How I Work

Calm triage, clean notes, and practical automation.

When something breaks

I verify the reported issue, review recent changes, and check the services and dependencies involved.

When something repeats

I turn the pattern into a checklist, script, dashboard, or documented workflow so the next response is faster and less fragile.

When risk is involved

I keep access, logging, change impact, and rollback paths visible. I document the checks needed before and after a change.

When I communicate

I write for the next engineer, the stakeholder, and my future self: what happened, what I checked, what changed, and what remains.
